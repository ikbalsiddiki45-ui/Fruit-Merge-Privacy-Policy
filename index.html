1. Introduction

Welcome to Fruit Merge ("we", "our", or "the Game"), created and distributed by Nexabytes Studio. We value your trust and are strongly committed to safeguarding the privacy and digital well-being of our players. This Privacy Policy explains the nature of information that may be processed when you install and enjoy Fruit Merge on your mobile device.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Zero Personal Identification Collection: We do NOT collect, harvest, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your real name, physical address, email address, phone number, or biometric data) through the Game.

A. Local On-Device Gameplay Data:

  • Your current high scores, best merge combinations, and unlock achievements.
  • Game pause/save states for resuming ongoing puzzle sessions.
  • Player audio and tactile settings (Sound Effects and Haptic Vibration toggles).

All gameplay state data is stored strictly locally on your own device using an internal Android SQLite Room Database. This data is never sent to our servers and can be wiped at any moment via the in-game Settings menu ("Reset High Score & Progress").

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

Fruit Merge requests only the minimal, necessary system permissions for core gameplay functionality:

  • Network Access (android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Enables the game to verify network connectivity, provide connection status recovery alerts, and serve in-game advertisements.
  • Haptic Feedback (android.permission.VIBRATE): Powers tactile physical vibration bumps when fruit circles drop, collide, and merge inside the jar.

4. Third-Party Advertising and Services

To provide Fruit Merge as a free-to-play mobile title, we incorporate Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display non-intrusive advertisements (Banner Ads, Interstitial Ads, and optional Rewarded Video Ads for game revival).

Third-party ad networks may collect anonymized device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), general IP address for regional ad delivery, and ad interaction metrics in compliance with applicable privacy frameworks.

You can learn more about Google's advertising policies and opt-out preferences here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Technologies & Policies

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Fruit Merge is a family-friendly casual puzzle game designed for audiences of all ages (Rated Everyone / PEGI 3). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

Our ad configurations are configured with child-directed treatment flags (TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT_TRUE) and maximum content rating constraints (MAX_AD_CONTENT_RATING_G) to prevent behavioral or personalized ad tracking for younger players.

6. Data Retention & User Control

Because all user data resides locally on your smartphone or tablet, you retain full ownership and control over your data:

  • In-Game Reset: Open the in-game Settings menu and select "Reset High Score & Progress" to purge all local scores and saved rounds.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application automatically deletes all local app sandboxed databases and cached files from your device.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect future features or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted here with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage players to review this page periodically.
